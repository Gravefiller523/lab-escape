HL2 FEEL: enemies and doors

The enemy pen (behind the ladder platform) now has a door. F2 refills the pen, F4 kills every enemy, F5 god mode, F7 locks or unlocks the pen door. The door course is to the right of the start.
1. Stand outside the shut pen door and make noise (sprint about, jump, or throw a crate at the door). The rats come to look: they push the door open, away from themselves, and come out.
2. Let them see you and run off. They follow you through doors, not round them.
3. Open the pen door yourself, then shut it with rats chasing you from inside: they push it open again.
4. Press F2 (rats back in the pen), then F7 to lock the door. Make noise outside: the rats can't open a locked door. F7 again unlocks it, and a moment later they come out.
5. Run through the door course with rats after you (F3 drops a kangaroo in front of you too). Doors 1 and 4 they push open. Door 2 is locked and door 3 is a gate (only the valve opens it), so they go round the end of those walls instead.
6. A door never swings into you while a rat opens it, and doors still swing away from you when you open them.
7. With a second player (Host local / Join local): the joiner sees the doors the rats open swing open too.
