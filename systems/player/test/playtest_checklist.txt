HL2 FEEL, STEP 4.3: the punt arm (punt, pull, shove)

Press F6 to get the punt arm (a robot arm on your right side); F6 again takes it away. The prop yard is ahead of you on the right; the enemy pen and keys F2 to F5 are still there.
1. Right click at a crate up to about 6 m away: it flies off hard, with a whump. Further away (or behind a wall), nothing happens.
2. Pick something up with E and right click: it's launched out of your hands, much faster than a left-click throw.
3. Light things (gems, the meat) fly faster than crates; the big heavy crate only shifts a bit.
4. Hold E on a crate further away (up to about 8 m): it is dragged towards you like a magnet, tumbling along the floor, and you pick it up. Let go of E early and it stops. The heavy crate can't be pulled.
5. With the arm you can also pick things up with E from a bit further away than before.
6. Right click a rat or the kangaroo (F2 refills the pen): it's shoved away and not hurt, but it comes after you. Rats fly, the kangaroo only stumbles.
7. Punt a crate at the rats: that still kills them. You can punt about twice a second, no faster.
8. With a second player (Host local / Join local, then F6 in the host window): both can punt and pull, and both see things fly.
