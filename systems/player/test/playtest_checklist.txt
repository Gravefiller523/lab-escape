HL2 FEEL, STEP 4: using things and doors

Turn round: the door course is behind you, on the right: four walls in a row, each with a door (look for the "door course" sign). The enemy pen and keys F2 to F5 are still there.
1. Press E at a wall or at nothing: a short "can't use that" buzz.
2. The tiny button on the post before the first wall: you don't have to aim exactly at it. It opens and shuts door 1.
3. Door 1 swings away from you, from either side. Press E again to shut it. The prompt only shows when you're close to its handle.
4. Stand in door 1's way while it shuts: it goes back open instead of shoving you. The crates behind it get pushed.
5. Door 2 is locked: E on it buzzes. The red button beside it opens it; it shuts after 3 s and locks itself again.
6. Door 3 is a gate you can't open by hand (buzz). Hold E on the red valve to turn it; let go early and it turns back. Fully turned, the gate opens, and shuts again once the valve has turned back.
7. Door 4 opens by itself when you walk up to it, and shuts a second after you leave.
8. With a second player (Host local / Join local): both see the doors and the valve move, and both can use them. Two people turning the valve go twice as fast.
