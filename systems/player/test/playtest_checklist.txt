HL2 FEEL, STEP 3: enemies like Half-Life 2

The enemy pen is behind the ladder platform (back left): a room with 4 rats and a kangaroo. Crates sit outside its door.
F2 puts the pen's enemies back. F3 drops a kangaroo 8 m in front of you. F4 kills every enemy. F5 is god mode.
1. Sneak up behind a rat (crouch): it doesn't see you. Step in front of it: a short pause, a chirp, then it comes. Its friends come too.
2. Crouch-walk near the pen: they don't hear you. Sprint past the door: they come out to look around.
3. Throw a crate into the pen: they come to see what made the noise.
4. Let the rats chase you, then duck behind the pillar or the platform: they run to where they last saw you and look around, not straight through walls.
5. Only two rats bite at once; the others circle about 3.5 m away. Every bite and kick has a short growl and wind-up first.
6. A kangaroo crouches, growls, then leaps. Throw a crate straight at a kangaroo (F3): it often jumps aside.
7. Kill a rat (throw crates, or F4): it tumbles away as a body instead of tipping over.
8. A pack moving together slides around each other instead of stacking up.
9. With a second player (Host local / Join local): the enemies split up between you.
